Graph 1: "Impact of Promotional Events on Average Discount (%)"
- X-Axis: Average discount percentage offered off MSRP.
- Y-Axis: Sales event category.

Key Findings:

- Big Billion Days leads all sales with a 37.0% average discount.
- Prime Day follows closely at 34.4%, with Great Indian Festival at 33.4% and Black Friday at 31.5%.
- On non-promotional Regular Days, the average discount sits at 20.3%.

Takeaway: Flash sales deliver an extra 11.2% to 16.7% price drop compared to standard days.

Graph 2: "Average Price Retention (%) by Product Category and Condition"
- X-Axis: Apple Product Category (Mac, Watch, iPad, iPhone).
- Y-Axis: Price Retention (% of original launch price).
- Colors: Dark Blue = New, Light Green = Renewed/Refurbished.

Key Findings:

- Macs are the strongest asset class, retaining 89.6% of launch price when brand new and 69.5% when refurbished.
- iPhones follow in second place, holding 85.1% (New) and 66.0% (Refurbished).
- Apple Watches and iPads depreciate fastest, both retaining 78.3% when new and dropping to ~60.5% for refurbished models.

Takeaway: High-end productivity hardware (Macs) retains value significantly longer than wearable devices or entry tablets.
